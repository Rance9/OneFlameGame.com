<p>One Flame is an original game for the NES by Rance9, made to run on the console itself as well as everywhere else NES games are played today. OneFlameGame.com is its home.</p><blockquote>Two children. One fire. A whole ice age to cross.</blockquote><h2>The last fire</h2><p>The cold came and did not leave. One by one the fires of the Eld went out, until a single torch was all that was left. Kesa and Brimi carry it out of the cave and across the ice, toward the Great Ember, the mountain that smokes. They were told one thing: <em>light every hearth you find.</em></p><figure><img src="/news/the-cave-of-the-eld.png" alt="The cave of the Eld, the elders around the fire"><figcaption>The cave of the Eld.</figcaption></figure><h2>One torch between two</h2><ul><li>Pass the torch when you're side by side, or throw it across a gap.</li><li>Feed the flame with wood. As it burns down, the circle of light shrinks with it.</li><li>The one holding the torch can barely fight. The other finds clubs, spears and axes along the trail.</li><li>Play together, one child each, or alone with the computer playing the other child.</li></ul><h2>Where you'll play it</h2><p>When it's out, One Flame will be free on Steam, including Steam Deck, and on iPhone, iPad and Android. You'll also be able to play it in Classic Cart Studio, on an NES Classic Edition, in an NES emulator, or on an NES cartridge on the original console.
