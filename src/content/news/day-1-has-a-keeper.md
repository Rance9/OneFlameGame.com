<p>Every day of the journey has a keeper standing between Kesa and Brimi and the way on. On the first day, it's <strong>Dousa</strong>.</p>
<p>Her fight is at dawn, on a river of clear ice. Her shadow swims under the children's feet the whole time, and the floor breaks into islands each time she comes up through it.</p>
<h2>The torch decides where she rises</h2>
<p>The fire's warmth thins the ice under whoever is carrying it. Stand still and that's where she comes up, so the one with the torch picks the spot.</p>
<blockquote>Keep moving, or choose where the ice gives way.</blockquote>
<h2>New on Day 1 this week</h2>
<ul>
<li>Her arena at the end of the river, with its own piece of music.</li>
<li>Ledges that crack and sink, and ice that thins under the torch.</li>
<li>A shadow under the river ice long before you meet her.</li>
</ul>
